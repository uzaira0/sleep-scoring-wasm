WEIGHTS LICENCE

The model weights shipped with this app (lstm_sleep_wake, teacher_* and
student_* .onnx files) are part of this application and are distributed under
its licence, the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0; see
LICENSE.txt). They were trained on, or distilled from models trained on,
spectral features derived from the three public datasets below. The datasets
themselves are not redistributed.

TRAINING DATA ATTRIBUTION

1. Newcastle
   van Hees, V., Charman, S., & Anderson, K. (2018).
   Newcastle polysomnography and accelerometer data (version 1.0). Zenodo.
   https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1160410
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2. Walch
   Walch, O. (2019).
   Motion and heart rate from a wrist-worn wearable and labeled sleep from polysomnography
   (version 1.0.0). PhysioNet.
   https://doi.org/10.13026/hmhs-py35
   Licence: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0 (ODC-By 1.0),
   https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

3. BIDSleep
   Song, T. (2026).
   A Multi-Night Instantaneous Heart Rate and Accelerometry Dataset with EEG Sleep Stage Labels
   (version 1.0.0). PhysioNet.
   https://doi.org/10.13026/a0sy-7t69
   Licence: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0 (ODC-By 1.0),
   https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

PhysioNet: Goldberger, A., Amaral, L., Glass, L., Hausdorff, J., Ivanov, P. C.,
Mark, R., ... & Stanley, H. E. (2000). PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and
PhysioNet: Components of a new research resource for complex physiologic
signals. Circulation, 101(23), e215-e220.

Changes: the recordings were converted to 30-second spectral features of
x, y, z and vector magnitude, and the networks were trained on those features.
No original recording is included in this app.
